# 🤔 About Me

I am Jing Qu, affiliated with Shandong University. My research focuses on human-computer interaction for healthcare, including virtual and augmented reality rehabilitation, multimodal neuroergonomics, fNIRS/EEG-based assessment, and data-driven design of intelligent rehabilitation systems.

My public research record is available through [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4783-9244) and [DBLP](https://dblp.org/pid/35/588-1.html). This website is initialized from public ORCID metadata; current position, email, location, and a formal profile photo are placeholders until updated.

# 🔥 News

- *2026*: Recent works listed on ORCID include PanoVR, ASAC-Net, knowledge graph-based rehabilitation design, and AR-enhanced multisensory calligraphy therapy.
- *2025*: Publications include work in IEEE TVCG, IEEE JBHI, IEEE IoT Journal, IJHCS, and related venues on VR/AR interaction, rehabilitation, and multimodal assessment.
- *2024*: Work on longitudinal VR training, fNIRS and eye-tracking assessment, and machine-learning-based brain function assessment appeared in public records.
