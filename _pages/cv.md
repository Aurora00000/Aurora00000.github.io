# Education

- Doctoral student, Shandong University, Jinan, China (2025 cohort).

# Research Experience

- Contributed core algorithms, system development, and application validation to the National Key R&D Program of China and other national and provincial research projects in artificial intelligence, brain-computer interfaces, and virtual reality rehabilitation.
- Conducted intelligent rehabilitation research for people with stroke, autism spectrum disorder, and cognitive impairment. Regular hospital and rehabilitation-center studies have involved more than **1,000 patient service encounters**.

# Research Interests

- Human-computer interaction for healthcare
- Virtual and augmented reality rehabilitation
- Multimodal neuroergonomics using fNIRS, EEG, eye tracking, and behavioral data
- Data-driven design and assessment of intelligent rehabilitation systems

# Research Output

- **22 academic papers** with Shandong University as the first affiliation: 10 first-author papers, 5 equal-first-author papers, and 4 second-author papers with my supervisor as first author.
- **11 papers** in CCF A venues or CAS Q1 Top journals; combined journal impact-factor total nearly 150.
- **7 invention patent applications**, including 4 granted patents; **2 software copyrights**.
